HOW I THINK

I design with curiosity, clarity & intent.

I don’t start with the screen. I start with the person, the context and the decision they need to make.

01

Understand before solving

I ask what people are trying to do, where they hesitate, and what matters in that moment.

02

Connect needs to goals

A useful interface should serve the user while moving the product toward a clear outcome.

03

Make the next step obvious

I use hierarchy, content and interaction to reduce cognitive load and keep the path easy to follow.

04

Make visuals earn their place

Typography, colour and motion should clarify the experience — not decorate it.
